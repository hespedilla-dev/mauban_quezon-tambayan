Hanna Kim F. Espedilla

A citizen of Mauban, Quezon.

Growing up in Mauban, Quezon, I always felt that our town’s charm wasn’t something you could fully capture on a standard tourist map. Visitors would pass through on their way to Cagbalete Island, missing the quiet beauty tucked away in our everyday corners—the historic stone streets, the breeze along the sea wall, and the cozy local spots where stories are shared over fresh food. As a local, I saw Mauban not just as a destination, but as a home filled with hidden gems that deserved to be appreciated. That inspiration led me to create the Mauban Quezon Tambayan website. I wanted to build a digital gathering space that looks at our town through the eyes of a citizen. Instead of just listing the usual commercial stops, the platform highlights authentic tambayans—from serene coastal views to warm, family-owned eateries—where both locals and travelers can pause, connect, and experience the genuine rhythm of Mauban. By mapping out these meaningful places, the project serves as a tribute to our community and an open invitation for everyone to discover the true heart of our hometown.
